# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A&M Health Clinic is a real full-body massage clinic at 98 Cleveland St, Fitzrovia,
central London (open late: Mon–Fri to 21:30, weekends to 21:00). Two core visitors:

1. **Stressed professionals** — people working in and around Soho, Fitzrovia, Marylebone
   and Mayfair who want to unwind after a long day. Their context: often on a phone, in the
   evening, looking to book a relaxing or deep-tissue session quickly and trust it's a
   legitimate, high-quality place. Emotional job: escape, calm, relief.

2. **Chronic pain / recovery seekers** — people dealing with back pain, tension, or injury
   who want real therapeutic work: deep tissue, cupping, acupuncture. Their job is results
   and confidence that the therapists are genuinely skilled. Emotional job: trust, relief,
   competence.

Both arrive wanting the same outcome: **to book a session** (online or by phone) with as
little friction and as much reassurance as possible.

## Product Purpose

A booking-driven marketing site for an existing clinic. It exists to convert a stressed or
in-pain Londoner into a booked appointment, while raising the clinic's perceived quality so
it reads as a premium, legitimate wellness destination, not a cheap parlour and not a cold
physio room. Success = more bookings and phone enquiries, and a brand that feels worth its
price. It replaces a generic, template-built site (unfilled "0+" stat counters, leftover
"Ginkgo Health Clinic" template copy) with something intentional and expensive-feeling.

## Brand Personality

**Premium · Discreet · Assured.** Quietly upscale, never salesy or flashy. Discretion
matters — massage is personal, so the tone is calm, respectful, and private, never
suggestive. Underneath the calm sits quiet expertise: real therapists, real technique, safe
hands. Voice: warm but restrained, plainspoken, confident. Think a high-end members' club or
boutique hotel spa, not a discount deal site and not a hospital. Emotional goals: calm,
trust, relief.

## Anti-references

- **Clinical & cold** — no sterile hospital/physio aesthetic, harsh whites, medical
  stiffness, or stock "doctor" imagery. Warmth over sterility.
- **Cheap massage parlour** — nothing neon, garish, seedy, or bargain-bin. No suggestive
  imagery or language. The site must read unambiguously legitimate and upscale; this is the
  single most important line to hold for a central-London massage brand.
- **Busy spa cliche** — no pink/lavender, lotus flowers, hot-stone stock photos, or
  cluttered discount banners.
- **Corporate & templated** — must not look like a SaaS or a website-builder template. The
  old site's generic, placeholder-ridden build is the exact thing we are moving away from.

## Design Principles

1. **Legitimacy first.** Every choice should reinforce that A&M is a real, professional,
   upscale clinic. When in doubt, choose the more discreet, more tasteful option.
2. **Calm is the product.** The site should itself feel like the relief it sells — spacious,
   still, unhurried. Restraint over noise.
3. **One clear path to booking.** Never bury the ask. A quiet but ever-present route to book
   or call, especially on mobile in the evening.
4. **Show real, don't claim.** Real prices, real services, real testimonials, real address.
   No fake counters, no empty superlatives. Concrete beats boastful.
5. **Serve both jobs.** Balance relaxation (escape, warmth) with therapeutic credibility
   (skill, results) so neither core client feels it's not for them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body text in both dark and light sections
(verified independently, not assumed). Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states.
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — keep the cinematic look but drop movement for those who
opt out. Clear, large tap targets and an obvious phone-booking option, since some clients
are older or less comfortable with online booking flows.
